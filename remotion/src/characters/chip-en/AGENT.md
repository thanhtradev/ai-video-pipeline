## Characters (the "Chip (YOU)" cast, English)

- The rig is `Character` from `kit/active/rig`: big glossy black round head with a thin cream rim
  light, two white oval eyes, NO mouth; expressions only through eye shapes (normal, `> <`,
  half-lid, wide, happy arcs, sad droop); blink and breathe. Hoodie with drawstrings, thin tube limbs,
  mitten hands. Kids are smaller with the same proportions.
- The main character "YOU" ALWAYS wears the saturated orange hoodie (`C.orange`). Everything else is
  muted and desaturated (blue-grey, dusty purple, olive, beige) and wrapped in `<Side>` so the act
  grade applies to it.
- Ready-made people in `kit/active/cast`: `You`, `Clone`, `Mom`, `Dad`, `Teacher` (cardigan, round
  glasses), `Elder` (grandparents, relatives), `Extra`. Add the episode's own people in
  `src/episode/kit.tsx` the same way. Never dress anyone in an áo dài or a conical hat.
- Names on screen (badges, papers, name tags) are the English names of the script.
