The narrator is "TÔI", a different ordinary person each episode (a student, a night-shift nurse, a motorbike-taxi driver, a daughter back in her mother's village…) who tells, in the first person and in the past tense, something that happened to them and that they still cannot explain; the voice is low, slow and close to the microphone, with long silences, and never performs or jokes. On screen every person is a black silhouette: TÔI is a tall thin shadow with two pale round eyes who carries an oil lamp; the other people of the story are smaller, dimmer shadows with everyday Vietnamese names; NÓ is the thing TÔI met: a taller shadow with arms past its knees and a tilted head, sometimes only two pale eyes in the dark, never explained, never named beyond "nó" or "bóng đó", never shown clearly or for long. Characters are listed with name and look (who the narrator is this time, what each shadow carries or wears as an outline, where NÓ stands); the main character is TÔI, the witness.
