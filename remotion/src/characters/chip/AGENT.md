## Characters (the "Chíp" cast)

- The rig is `Character` from `kit/active/rig`: big glossy black round head with a thin cream rim
  light, two white oval eyes, NO mouth; expressions only through eye shapes (normal, `> <`,
  half-lid, wide, happy arcs, sad droop); blink and breathe. Hoodie with drawstrings, thin tube limbs,
  mitten hands. Kids are smaller with the same proportions.
- The main character "BẠN" ALWAYS wears the saturated orange hoodie (`C.orange`). Everything else is
  muted and desaturated (blue-grey, dusty purple, olive, beige) and wrapped in `<Side>` so the act
  grade applies to it.
- Ready-made people in `kit/active/cast`: `Ban` (you), `Clone`, `Me` (mum), `Ba` (dad), `Co`
  (teacher), `Extra`, `Relative`. Add the episode's own people in `src/episode/kit.tsx` the same way.
